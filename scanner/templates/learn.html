What is a company worth? Four ideas, one lemonade stand.

1 · What makes a business worth something?

The spare cash it makes for its owners.

Sell $500 of lemonade, pay $250 for supplies and $150 for help: $100 is left. Investors call it free cash flow. Every company on the list keeps at least 15¢ of each $1 of sales this way.

2 · Is the price a good deal?

Pay $1,000 for the stand and its $100 a year is 10¢ back for every $1. A safe bond pays .

The more you pay, the less cash you get back per $1. Every company page starts with this number.

3 · Then why would anyone pay $3,000?

Every price hides a bet like this. Each company page shows the bet next to how fast the business really grew.

4 · Does any of this work?

Tested month by month since 2014, using only what people knew at the time. Most high-rate months are 2022 onward, so it’s a pattern, not a promise. It also can’t tell you whether a company’s problems are temporary.
